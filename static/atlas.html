"Matched on map" is computed here, right now, by tying CMS's per-facility hospital layer back to each operator's roster — treat it as approximate; renamed, newly built, or non-Medicare hospitals won't match. Neither column shows bed capacity or patient volume: CMS's Hospital General Information dataset doesn't publish bed counts, and no public source reports annual patient counts per operator in a comparable, verifiable way — better to omit both than guess.
