About this tracker

This is an independent, unofficial tracking tool created by Bobby Hong (bobbyhong.com). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, CMS, or any state government agency named on this site.

Data & accuracy

Information is compiled from publicly available government sources and news coverage, cited throughout. It may be incomplete, delayed, or contain errors — always verify directly with the relevant state agency before making any funding, application, or business decision based on this data. Entries marked "unverified lead" come from secondary sources or an official page that couldn't be directly confirmed, and should be treated with extra caution.

Editorial content

The Pros/Cons, "How this state compares," and Program Overview sections, and the Commentary page, contain analysis, summaries, and third-party opinion — not official guidance, and not the views of any state program or agency. They're provided to aid comparison, not as a recommendation.

Not professional advice

Nothing on this site constitutes legal, financial, or grant-writing advice. Consult qualified professionals and the official program materials before applying for or relying on any funding described here.
